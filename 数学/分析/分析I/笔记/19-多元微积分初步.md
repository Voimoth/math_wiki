# 19 多元微积分初步

## 多元微积分基础

$f: D\subseteq\mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$，$D$ 是 $\mathbb{R}^n$ 中的开集
$(x_1,\cdots,x_n) \mapsto f(x_1,\cdots,x_n)$
$n$ 个偏导数 $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1},\cdots,\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}$

其中 $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(x_0) = \lim_{h\to 0}\frac{f(x_0+he_i)-f(x_0)}{h}$

梯度 $\nabla f(x_0) = \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(x_0),\cdots,\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}(x_0)\right)$ 表示增长最快的方向

例.
$$\frac{d}{dt}f(x_1(t),x_2(t)) = \lim_{\Delta t\to 0}\frac{f(x_1(t+\Delta t),\ x_2(t+\Delta t))-f(x_1(t),x_2(t))}{\Delta t}$$
$$= \lim_{\Delta t\to 0}\frac{f(x_1(t)+x_1'(t)\Delta t+o(\Delta t),\ x_2(t)+x_2'(t)\Delta t+o(\Delta t))-f(x_1(t),x_2(t))}{\Delta t}$$
$$= \lim_{\Delta t\to 0}\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(x_1,x_2)\frac{\Delta x_1}{\Delta t} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}(x_1,x_2)\frac{\Delta x_2}{\Delta t} + o(1)$$
$$= \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}\frac{dx_1}{dt} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}\frac{dx_2}{dt}$$

存在唯一切面
$\Downarrow$
可导 $\nRightarrow$ 可微
可用切面局部线性近似

即：
$$f(x_1+\Delta x_1,\ x_2+\Delta x_2) = f(x_1,x_2) + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(x_1,x_2)\Delta x_1 + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}(x_1,x_2)\Delta x_2 + o\!\left(\sqrt{\Delta x_1^2+\Delta x_2^2}\right)$$

## 高阶导数与混合偏导数

微分：$df = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}dx_1 + \cdots + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}dx_n$
高阶导数：$\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1\partial x_2} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_2\partial x_1}$

**证明：** 在 $(a,b)$ 点，
记 $\Delta(h,k) = f(a+h,b+k) - f(a+h,b) - (f(a,b+k)-f(a,b))$
令 $\varphi(x) = f(x,b+k) - f(x,b)$
$\Rightarrow \Delta(h,k) = \varphi(a+h)-\varphi(a) = \varphi'(a+\theta_1 h)h$
$= h\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(a+\theta_1 h,\ b+k) - h\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(a+\theta_1 h,\ b)$
$= kh\frac{\partial}{\partial x_2}\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}(a+\theta_1 h,\ b+\theta_2 k)$
令 $\psi(y) = f(a+h,y) - f(a,y)$
$\Rightarrow \Delta(h,k) = kh\frac{\partial}{\partial x_1}\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}(a+\tilde\theta_1 h,\ b+\tilde\theta_2 k)$
令 $h,k\to 0 \Rightarrow \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1\partial x_2} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_2\partial x_1}$

## Laplace 算子与调和函数

$\Delta f = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1^2} + \cdots + \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_n^2}$

称 $f$ 为调和函数，若 $\Delta f = 0$。
如 $\mathbb{R}^3$ 中 $\Delta\frac{1}{r} = 0$；
$\mathbb{R}^2$ 中 $\Delta\log r = 0$（$r = \sqrt{x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2}$）

## 坐标系

$\mathbb{R}^2$ 中 $\begin{cases} x = r\cos\theta \\ y = r\sin\theta \end{cases}$ 即有 $f: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$，$(r,\theta) \mapsto (x,y)$
$$Df_{(r_0,\theta_0)} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial x}{\partial r} & \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} \\ \frac{\partial y}{\partial r} & \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta} \end{pmatrix}\Bigg|_{(r_0,\theta_0)}$$

## 向量场

$D\subseteq\mathbb{R}^3$，$\boldsymbol{v}: D\to\mathbb{R}^3$
$x \mapsto \boldsymbol{v}(x) = (v_1(x),v_2(x),v_3(x))$

散度 $\mathrm{div}\,\vec v = \frac{\partial v_1}{\partial x_1} + \frac{\partial v_2}{\partial x_2} + \frac{\partial v_3}{\partial x_3} = \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x_1},\frac{\partial}{\partial x_2},\frac{\partial}{\partial x_3}\right)\cdot(v_1,v_2,v_3) = \nabla\cdot\vec v$

旋度
$$\nabla\times\vec v = \begin{vmatrix} e_1 & e_2 & e_3 \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial x_1} & \frac{\partial}{\partial x_2} & \frac{\partial}{\partial x_3} \\ v_1 & v_2 & v_3 \end{vmatrix}$$

## 若干恒等式

$$\int_{\Omega} \operatorname{div}\vec v\,\mathrm{d}x = \int_{\partial\Omega} \vec v\cdot\vec n\,\mathrm{d}S$$

$$\nabla(fg) = f\nabla g + g\nabla f$$
$$\nabla\cdot(f\vec v) = \nabla f\cdot\vec v + f\,\nabla\cdot\vec v$$
$$\nabla\times\nabla f = 0$$
$$\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\vec v) = 0$$

## 隐函数定理

已知 $f(x,y)=0$
什么时候 $f(x,y(x))=0$?
当 $\frac{\partial}{\partial y}f(x_0,y_0) \neq 0$ 时。

若
$$\begin{cases} f_1(x_1,\cdots,x_n) = 0 \\ \vdots \\ f_m(x_1,\cdots,x_n) = 0 \end{cases} \quad ?$$
$$\begin{vmatrix} \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial x_{m+1}} & \cdots & \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial x_n} \\ \vdots & & \vdots \\ \frac{\partial f_m}{\partial x_{m+1}} & \cdots & \frac{\partial f_m}{\partial x_n} \end{vmatrix} \neq 0$$
